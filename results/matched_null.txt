=== INTERPRETATION ===
  The anchor performs WORSE against the real cohort than against a
  same-population null of identical size. Its apparent ability to predict
  replication is therefore NOT evidence that it detects cross-cohort
  artefacts: an AUC of this magnitude arises with no artefact present at all.
  Table 4's AUCs must be reported against this null, not against 0.5.

  Note the replication RATE does separate the arms (0.956 null vs 0.845 real):
  the cohorts genuinely differ. It is the ANCHOR'S AUC that fails to register it.
